# The Haven — Brand Kit

![Brand sheet](brand-sheet.png)

The Haven is a Christ-centred campus ministry at the Copperbelt University. The identity is about **shelter and light**: a safe place to belong, and the light of God's Word in it.

## Logos

| Folder | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `logo-icon/` | **Primary logo.** The symbol next to the wordmark, horizontal or stacked | App screens, website headers, posters, letterheads |
| `logo-text/` | **Text-only logo.** The "The Haven" wordmark, horizontal or stacked | Narrow spaces, merch print, places where the symbol already appears |
| `symbol/` | The symbol on its own | Social avatars, stickers, watermarks |
| `monogram/` | "H." monogram | Favicons, embroidery, very small sizes |
| `app-icon/` | App icon (1024 px) and Android adaptive foreground | App stores and the mobile app build |

Each folder has `svg/` files (vector, transparent background; use these when you can) and `png/` files (on a solid background, for quick use).

### The symbol
An open Bible turned like a roof becomes a **shelter**. Underneath it a **gold light** burns, and a ground line steadies it. The symbol stands for Scripture, refuge and light.

### The wordmark
"The" is set in Fraunces Italic in Lamp Gold. "Haven" is set in Fraunces Bold in Haven Navy, or White on dark backgrounds. The letters are converted to outlines, so the SVGs look the same on every machine.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Haven Navy | `#0f2d6e` | Primary colour: headers, buttons, logo |
| Lamp Gold | `#d9a441` | Accent only: the light, "The", highlights. Never use it for body text. |
| Night | `#0B0F12` | Dark backgrounds and body text |
| Linen | `#fafbfc` | Page background |
| White | `#ffffff` | Cards, and text on navy or night |

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Fraunces Bold. Pair it with Fraunces Italic for a small lead-in word, like the "The" in the wordmark.
- **Body and UI:** the system sans-serif (SF Pro on iOS, Roboto on Android) or Inter.
- The fonts are in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the gold light on all sides.
- ✅ Use the white logo on Navy or Night, and the navy logo on Linen or White.
- ❌ Don't recolour the gold light, stretch the logo, add shadows, or put it on busy photos without a navy overlay.
- ❌ Don't set "The Haven" in any other typeface as a logo.
